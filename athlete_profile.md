### Practical Session Examples

**Easy Run (60 min, 10–11 km)**
- Pace: 5:30–5:50 /km
- Conversational; settle into comfortable rhythm; acceptable to run solo or with group at recovery pace
- Recovery marker: HR should drop to resting levels within 2 hours post-run

**Long Run (90–100 min, ~16–18 km)**
- Structure: 20 min warm-up E-pace → 60 min steady M-pace (4:46/km) → 10 min cool-down E-pace
- Target: 3–4 M-pace runs before race week
- Achilles check: If stiffness worsens, dial back to full E-pace; skip M-segment entirely in deload weeks

**Threshold/Tempo Run (50–55 min, ~9–10 km)**
- Structure: 10 min easy warm-up → 2–3 × 5–7 min T-pace (4:28/km) with 2 min jog recovery → 10 min cool-down
- Frequency: 1 × per week (Tuesday or Wednesday), never back-to-back with long run
- **Achilles caution:** Tempo pace is high enough to load the Achilles. Skip if pain escalates to Yellow/Red zone

**Interval Workout (45–50 min, ~9 km)**
- Structure: 10 min warm-up → 4–5 × 4 min I-pace (4:06/km) with 1 min jog recovery → 10 min cool-down
- Frequency: 1 × per week (Saturday or Sunday), after full recovery day
- **Achilles caution:** High-intensity intervals increase Achilles stress. Prioritize if pain is Green zone only

**Short Strides (10 min total)**
- 8–10 × 80–100 m at R-pace (3:49/km or just under 10K effort) with 60–90 sec jog recovery
- Neuromuscular "wake-up" after easy runs; keeps fast-twitch recruitment sharp
- Low Achilles risk if paced conservatively

## 3. Achilles Tendinopathy & Workload Management

*Sources: **achilles_acwr_rehab.md** | **polarized_80_20_seiler.md***

### Pain Monitoring Framework (Silbernagel & Alfredson Model)

| **Zone** | **Pain Range** | **Next-Morning Stiffness** | **Action** |
|----------|---|---|---|
| **Green** | 0–3/10 during session, returns to baseline <24h | Minimal or settles with warm-up | ✅ Continue E-pace runs; carefully trial T/I sessions; monitor ACWR |
| **Yellow/Red** | 3–4+/10 or sharp twinge; worsening stiffness next morning | Persists >24h or requires extended warm-up | 🔴 **PAUSE:** Remove all speed work (T/I/R) for 7–14 days; switch to easy flat running or cross-training |

### Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) Safety Rules

*Formula: `ACWR = Acute Load (last 7 days km) / Chronic Load (rolling 28-day avg weekly km)`*

**Current Baseline (Assumed Steady State):**
- Weekly volume: 38 km/week
- Chronic load (28-day rolling avg): ~38 km/week
- **Baseline ACWR:** 1.0 (safe)

**Safe Progression:**
| **ACWR Range** | **Status** | **Weekly Volume** | **Action** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.8–1.3** | ✅ **Sweet Spot** | 30.4–49.4 km | Optimal adaptation; low injury spike risk. Continue current progression. |
| **1.3–1.5** | ⚠️ **Caution** | 49.4–57 km | Elevated tendon stress. Avoid adding volume AND intensity simultaneously. Extend recovery week. |
| **>1.5** | 🔴 **Danger** | >57 km | High Achilles spike risk. Cut back immediately: reduce weekly volume 10–15%, skip one interval session, add cross-training. |
| **<0.8** | ⚠️ **Undertraining** | <30.4 km | Tissue capacity may drop if sustained >2 weeks. After injury recovery, rebuild at 5–8% per week. |

### Achilles-Specific Conditioning

**If Pain Remains Green (Recommended 2–3 × per week):**

1. **Isometric Calf Holds** (Analgesia; Pain Reduction)
   - 5 × 45 sec mid-range (knee bent or straight) standing or seated
   - Performed on non-run days or after easy runs (>6 hrs post-run to avoid cumulative load)
   - Goal: Tolerance to load without increasing pain

2. **Heavy Slow Resistance (HSR) Calf Raises** (Tissue Adaptation)
   - Straight-knee calf raises: 3 × 8–12 reps (bodyweight or weighted)
   - Bent-knee soleus work: 3 × 8–12 reps (targets deep plantarflexor)
   - Frequency: 2–3 × per week (Mon/Wed/Fri or similar)
   - Progression: Add 2.5–5 kg per week if tolerated pain-free
   - Timing: Off-run days or 48+ hrs after long run / interval session

**If Pain Escalates to Yellow/Red:**
- **STOP all tempo (T), interval (I), and repetition (R) work immediately**
- Substitute 1–2 × per week pool running or stationary cycling (60–80% HRmax) for lost intensity
- Maintain 4–5 × per week easy flat running at E-pace (pain must stay ≤2/10)
- Resume HSR calf work 3 × per week (consistent, controlled load)
- **Seek sports physio evaluation if pain persists >7–10 days**

**Weeks 5–7: Intensity & Endurance Build**

**Week 8: Deload / Recovery Week**
- Objective: Peak recovery before race-prep block
- Volume: **–20–25%** → 33 km
- Easy runs + strides only
- Long run: 13 km easy pace
- Achilles: Full recovery; intensive HSR calf work (3 × per week)
- ACWR: ~0.85

**Weeks 9–11: Race-Prep & Tempo Block**
- Objective: Sharpen 1:38 HM pace, peak fitness
- Achilles priority: Cautious; avoid sudden spikes in T/I volume
- Intensity: 1 tempo (sustained 20–30 min T-pace or 4–5 × 5 min) + 1 interval (4–5 × 4 min) per week
- Long run: Maintain 17–18 km with 60–70 min M-pace core (this IS your race rehearsal)
- Mid-week run: 1 × progressive 10 km run (start easy, finish at M-pace) to simulate race-day conditions
- ACWR: 1.1–1.15 (peak stimulus)
- Weekly volume: 42 → 43 → 42 km

**Week 12: Taper & Rest**
- Objective: Maximize freshness for race day
- Volume: **–35–40%** → 26 km
- Short, easy runs only (3–4 × 5 km)
- 1 × very short tempo primer: 2 × 3 min at race pace to maintain neuromuscular sharpness
- NO long run; NO hard intervals
- Achilles: Minimal running stress; mobilization + stretching focus
- ACWR: ~0.65 (rest)

## 7. Supplementary Work & Recovery Protocols

### Daily Mobility (5–10 min)
- **Pre-run:** Dynamic leg swings, walking lunges, arm circles, gradual pace increase (never cold hard efforts)
- **Post-run:** Walking cool-down (3–5 min), then static stretches (calf, hip flexor, glute, quad, hamstring) for 20–30 sec each; hold light tension, no bouncing
- **Achilles-specific:** 2–3 × daily gentle calf stretches (doorway or wall); avoid aggressive stretching if pain is Yellow/Red

### Sleep & Nutrition
- **Sleep target:** 7–9 hours per night (non-negotiable for recovery, tendon remodeling, Achilles adaptation)
- **Carbohydrate:** 5–7 g/kg body weight per day for 40+ km weeks (fuel aerobic runs, enable quality intensity)
- **Protein:** 1.6–2.0 g/kg per day (tendon collagen synthesis)
- **Hydration:** 500–750 ml per hour during runs >60 min; electrolyte tablet in hot weather

### Cross-Training (If Achilles Flares)
- **Cycling:** 45–60 min steady (Zone 1–2) on stationary bike; zero impact on Achilles
- **Pool running:** Suspended aqua-jogging; mimics running mechanics; zero ground impact
- **Swimming:** Low intensity (recovery strokes); avoid aggressive flip turns (Achilles load)
