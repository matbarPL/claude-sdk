## Achilles Injury Management (Silbernagel Model)
*Source: **achilles_acwr_rehab.md***

**Pain Zones:**
- **Green (0–3/10):** Mild stiffness settling during run, baseline <24h → Continue running + calf strengthening
- **Yellow/Red (>3–4/10 or sharp twinge):** Worsening next-morning stiffness → Pause all T/I/R work for 7–14 days

**Conditioning (3× per week if Green):**
1. **Isometric Calf Holds:** 5×45 sec mid-range (non-run days)
2. **HSR Calf Raises:** 3×8–12 straight-knee + 3×8–12 bent-knee soleus (Mon/Wed/Fri)

**If Yellow/Red Escalates:**
- Eliminate T/I/R pace entirely; switch to easy flat + cross-training (pool, cycling)
- Increase HSR frequency to 3× per week
- Seek sports physio if pain persists >7 days

## ACWR Safety (Gabbett Framework)

**Formula:** Acute Load (7d km) ÷ Chronic Load (28d avg weekly km) = ACWR

| ACWR | Status | Action |
|------|--------|--------|
| **0.8–1.3** | ✅ Sweet spot | Optimal adaptation; continue |
| **1.3–1.5** | ⚠️ Caution | Avoid adding volume + intensity simultaneously |
| **>1.5** | 🔴 Danger | Cut volume 10–15%, skip 1 speed session, add cross-train |

**12-Week ACWR Plan:**
```
Weeks 1–3: 1.0–1.1 | Week 4: 0.8 | Weeks 5–7: 1.1–1.15 | Week 8: 0.85 
Weeks 9–11: 1.1–1.15 | Week 12: 0.65
```

## Key Rules

✅ **Easy pace is primary:** 5:26–5:58/km, conversational  
✅ **Tempo capped:** 20–30 min T-pace per session max  
✅ **Intervals capped:** ≤3.2 km total per week  
✅ **Deload every 4th week:** –20–25% volume  
✅ **Achilles is gatekeeper:** Green→Yellow = drop intensity immediately  
✅ **Resting HR:** Track AM baseline; rise 3–5 bpm = fatigue signal  
✅ **Aerobic decoupling:** ≤5% (HR rise vs pace over 90 min) = strong base
